# Adaptive Pathway v0.1 — Samsung LEO Venture

The robustness matrix does not say "do GaaS." It says: **the plan-as-written is a wager on W2, and the rational structure is a staged policy that buys world-information cheaply before committing capital irreversibly.** The memo's own Phase 1 ($1.5–2.5M) is that purchase — an option premium of ~$0.0025B against a worst-case regret of $7.15B. This document turns the matrix into the policy.

## 1. The policy, stated

Default posture: **no constellation capex authorized.** Run Phase 1 as a world-discrimination instrument. The memo's Phase-1 gates, reinterpreted, are not pass/fail tests of the plan — they are **measurements of which axis pole we live on**: the launch gate (<$2,000/kg from ≥2 providers) measures the supply axis; the LOI/co-invest gate (premium ≥15% over GEO VSAT, 50/50 gateway term sheets) measures the demand axis. Two gates, two axes, four outcomes, four prepared responses.

## 3. Robust act-now moves (no-regret, all four worlds)

From the cross-world risk sort: **R7** COTS radiation test program (kill-condition in every world — start immediately, it is also the longest-lead item); **R10** anchor aerospace hires (constrains every world); **R8** ITAR/EAR formal audit (HIGH in 3 of 4 worlds; cheap, decisive); **R12** organizational separation from consumer-electronics product cycles (model-external, world-independent). These spend small money now regardless of which world materializes — the definition of a no-regret move.

## 4. The loop — what happens after Phase 1 reports (V2 re-instantiation)

Phase 1 ending is not the end of the analysis; it is the trigger for **re-instantiating** it. Two things change, exactly as anticipated:

**The X-space shrinks.** Parameters cross the tag boundary from deep-uncertain toward estimated: launch quotes turn P5 into a number with a confidence band; LOI conversations turn P19 (the premium) into evidence; radiation results turn R7 into a fact. In levels language: pieces of the problem demote from level 3 to level 2 — where ordinary analysis (Innovera's existing engine) takes over. The worlds are then **redrawn on the residual deep uncertainty**: axis selection recurs each cycle, because yesterday's axes may be partially resolved and what remains deep may be different (e.g., once entry is committed, *competitor response* becomes a candidate axis that did not exist pre-entry).

**The L-space shrinks.** Irreversible commitments convert levers into constants. If path X is taken and path Y becomes unreachable, then regret in V2 is computed **over the reachable set only** — path-dependent regret. The matrix is re-run on the strategies that still exist, against the worlds that remain possible.

This is the explore-exploit structure, legitimately applied at last: each stage *explores* (spends a bounded budget to buy information that cannot be obtained any other way — you cannot search the web for your own pilot results), each gate *exploits* (commits irreversibly on the basis of what was learned), and the cycle repeats on what remains unknown. Two honesty notes carry forward: the funnel narrows but never closes (level-4 residue is permanent, and new deep uncertainties are born as the venture acts on the world); and there is no learned transition function — each re-instantiation is re-framed by judgment, which is why axis selection stays the expert step.

**Product implication (Innovera).** If the analysis re-instantiates on every gate, the deliverable is not a memo — it is a **subscription to the loop**: signpost monitoring between gates, re-instantiation at each gate, a living robustness matrix. The memo is the entry ticket; the loop is the product.
